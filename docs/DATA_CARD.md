## 1. Overview
| | Synthetic cohort (training and testing) | CGMacros (external check only) |
|---|---|---|
| Source | Generated by our own code, `glucotwin/synthetic.py` | PhysioNet, CGMacros v1.0.0, open access |
| Real people? | No | Yes, de-identified by the data owners |
| Role | Train, validate and test the forecaster; drive the what-if simulator | Test of the forecaster only (experiments A and B) |

## 2. Synthetic data
### 2.1 How it is generated
* `make_ehr(n_patients=150, seed=42)`: Synthea-style static records for a T2D / pre-diabetic Indian cohort.
* `simulate_patient(...)`: a compact mechanistic simulator that produces 14 days of 5-minute data per patient (288 samples per day). Patient `i` uses seed `42 * 1000 + i`. Recording starts on 2026-01-05.
* `generate_cohort(150, 14, 42)` combines both into the full dataset.

### 2.2 Static EHR stream
Demographics (age, sex, BMI), HbA1c, fasting glucose, blood pressure, LDL, triglycerides, eGFR, years since diagnosis, family history, TCF7L2 risk-allele count (0 to 2), diet (veg / non-veg), activity level, medications (metformin, sulfonylurea), prior CVD, hypertension, and ICD-10 diagnosis codes (for example E11.9, I10, I25.1, N18.3, E66.9). Values are drawn from simple distributions with clinically motivated dependencies (for example HbA1c rises with BMI, disease duration and risk alleles; fasting glucose follows the ADA eAG formula).

### 2.3 Dynamic wearable stream (5-minute resolution)
| Column | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `glucose` | CGM glucose, mg/dL (clipped to 55 to 400) |
| `hr` | heart rate, bpm |
| `hrv_rmssd` | heart-rate variability (RMSSD), ms |
| `steps` | steps per 5-minute bin |
| `sleep_stage` | -1 awake / not in bed, 0 awake in bed, 1 light, 2 deep, 3 REM |

A separate meals table records meal time, type and carbohydrate grams (ground truth, not a model input).

### 2.4 Hidden simulator state
`ehr_with_latent_truth.csv` also contains `latent_ir` (insulin resistance), `latent_walk_habit` and `latent_rest_hr`. These drive the simulator and are **not part of the public EHR and not model inputs**. They exist so that the what-if tool and the calibration check can be evaluated against known truth. In `VirtualPatient.what_if`, IR can be estimated from CGM (`calibrate_ir`); walk habit, resting HR, meal timings and the noise seed are still the true values.

### 2.5 Physiology encoded (simplified)
Fasting level anchored to HbA1c; post-meal excursion scaling with carbohydrate load and insulin resistance; short sleep raising next-day insulin resistance; stress raising glucose and lowering HRV; walking after meals blunting the excursion; Indian meal patterns (rice, roti, idli; vegetarian vs non-vegetarian). These are literature-inspired assumptions, not fitted to real data.

### 2.6 Label
Event = glucose above 180 mg/dL at any time in the next 120 minutes, evaluated only at times when current glucose is 160 mg/dL or lower. Prevalence among eligible forecast times is about 17%.

### 2.7 Splits
Patient-level 70 / 15 / 15 train / validation / test, repeated 3 times. No patient appears in two splits.

### 2.8 Known limitations of the synthetic data
* Labels come from our own simulator, so metrics show how well the pipeline learns a known process, not clinical accuracy.
* The simulator is simplified: no illness, no insulin injections, no alcohol, no travel, no sensor drift or dropouts, regular meal schedules.
* In this simulation, HRV, sleep and steps have little predictive value for the forecast, which may not hold in real life.
* Demographics are invented, so no subgroup fairness analysis is possible.
* Not validated against any specific real cohort for EHR realism.

## 3. External real data: CGMacros v1.0.0
* **Use:** evaluation of the forecaster only. 45 adults (15 healthy, 16 prediabetes, 14 T2D by HbA1c), about 10 days each, 25,306 eligible forecast times, event prevalence 12.1%.
* **Sensor:** Dexcom G6 Pro is the primary glucose source (chosen in advance). Libre Pro readings were used only for a sensitivity check.
* **Features used:** CGM-derived and time-of-day features, plus 7 EHR-like fields shared with our schema in one experiment. HRV, sleep and steps do not exist in this dataset.
* **Access:** download from https://physionet.org/content/cgmacros/1.0.0/ and follow the licence on that page. Place `bio.csv` and the participant folders in `data/real/cgmacros/`.
* **Licence and citation:** `<LICENCE TEXT AND CITATION AS STATED ON THE PHYSIONET PAGE>`
* **Limits:** one site, small sample, mostly mild T2D, one CGM brand as the primary source.

## 4. Privacy and ethics
* The training data is generated by our code; no personal data is processed for training.
* CGMacros is de-identified and open access. We do not redistribute it and make no attempt to re-identify participants.
* No clinical data, patient contact or hospital data was used. Any future use of clinical data would need ethics approval and compliance with applicable law (for example India's DPDP Act).

## 5. How to regenerate
```bash
python scripts/run_pipeline.py --quick     # small cohort
python scripts/run_pipeline.py             # full 150-patient cohort -> data/synthetic/
```
Outputs: EHR (with and without latent truth), 5-minute wearable series, meal ground truth.
